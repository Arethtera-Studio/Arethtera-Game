# Epic 7 - Define handling of very short offline periods

## Objective: 
Determine what happens when the player leaves for a very short period.

## Issues

### 7.1 — Define Short Offline Threshold
Determine what qualifies as "very short."

### 7.2 — Determine Whether Short Offline Time Is Processed
Determine whether even a few seconds/minutes are processed.

### 7.3 — Define Minimum Action Progress
Determine whether actions can make progress during very short periods.

### 7.4 — Define Resource Changes During Short Offline Periods
Determine whether fractional resource changes are possible.

### 7.5 — Define Short Offline Rounding
Determine whether short periods are rounded or processed exactly.

### 7.6 — Create Short Offline Examples
Examples:
5 seconds
30 seconds
1 minute
5 minutes
15 minutes

### 7.7 — Document Short Offline Rules

## Epic 7 Deliverable
A defined and testable behavior for short offline periods.
